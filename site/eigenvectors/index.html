Find the arrows a matrix only stretches, and meet PCA’s directions again.

1

A matrix moves arrows

Multiply an arrow by a 2 × 2 matrix and you get a new arrow: usually turned and stretched.

2

Some arrows don’t turn

A few special directions come out pointing the same way, only longer or shorter. Those are eigenvectors.

3

The stretch is the eigenvalue

How much an eigenvector is stretched is its eigenvalue. For a spread matrix it is the spread of the shadows: PCA again.
